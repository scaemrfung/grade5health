Grade 5 Health — Growth and Development

From: Patrick Fung, Grade 5
Re: Upcoming PEW lessons on maturation, puberty, and reproductive capacity
When: Weeks 25–30 of our Health block (about six 30-minute lessons)

Dear families,

Alberta’s Grade 5 Physical Education and Wellness curriculum includes two outcomes in Growth and Development:

  • Students investigate maturation and identify changes during adolescence.
  • Students connect puberty to the capacity for human reproduction.

I will teach this in a factual, calm, and respectful way. Lessons are 30 minutes. Students will use correct anatomical words (for example: penis, testicles, vulva, vagina, uterus, ovaries). We will cover physical, thinking, and behaviour changes; hormones; menstruation and sperm production as signs of reproductive capacity; and positive health practices (sleep, nutrition, activity, hygiene, trusted adults).

What we will not teach in Grade 5: the fertilization-through-pregnancy-and-birth sequence (that is Grade 6). We will not teach sexual activity or dating advice. Students are never asked to share private information about their own bodies.

Alberta’s Education Act requires notice and allows you to opt your child out of human sexuality instruction. If you opt out, your child will complete an alternate wellness task in a supervised space. This is not a punishment and will not be announced to the class.

Please return the slip below. If I do not receive a slip, I will follow EIPS procedure for contacting you before this unit begins.

Questions: contact me at school or through the office. You are welcome to preview the lessons on our class Health pages.

Thank you,
Patrick Fung
Grade 5 · Elk Island Public Schools
