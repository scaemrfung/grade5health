Assessment tools

Tools for assessing the six units with the EIPS Grades 3–6 levels. No numbers or percentages. This site saves rubric scores only on the device you use; PowerTeacher Pro is the mark book.

Class checklist

Printable whole-class observation and conversation checklist, one page per unit, with columns for each summative piece. Blank class list.

Second-chance tasks

One short reassessment task per unit, tied to the same outcome, with look-fors.

Alternate wellness task

Six sessions for students without a signed opt-in for Growth & Development (Weeks 25–30).

Summative pieces by unit

  • Character Development: Week 6 (product)
  • Healthy Relationships: Week 11 (observation) and Week 12 (product)
  • Safety: Week 17 (product) and a Week 18 consent conversation
  • Healthy Eating: Week 24 (product)
  • Growth & Development: Week 27 and Week 30 (products), opted-in students only
  • Financial Literacy: Week 36 (product)

All other weekly sheets are formative practice.
