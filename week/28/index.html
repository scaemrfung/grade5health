Menstruation and Sperm Production

Signs of reproductive capacity · 30 minutes

Unit packet

30-minute block

05:00Warm-up

Today’s focus. Menstruation and sperm production are signs that the reproductive system is maturing. Teach the processes factually. Hygiene and products, calmly. Not sexual activity.

Materials

  • Week 28 worksheet
  • Question box
  • Optional: unopened pad (still in wrapper) as a visual — no demo on a body
5 min

Warm-up

Reset the science-voice rule. Today’s words: menstruation, period, ovulation, sperm, semen, hygiene. Giggles get one reset.

8 min

Two signs of capacity

  1. 1Menstruation: hormones help the uterus grow a lining. An ovary may release an egg (ovulation). If that egg is not going to become a pregnancy, the lining leaves the body through the vagina — that is a period. Blood and tissue. It can last several days. First periods are often irregular. This is a sign the system is maturing.
  2. 2People who menstruate can use a pad (sits in underwear), a tampon (goes in the vagina — name it, do not practise), or other products families choose. Change them, wash hands, and tell a trusted adult if pain is intense or if they are unprepared at school. Schools have a plan for that — know yours.
  3. 3Sperm production: testicles make sperm cells. Sperm travels through the vas deferens and mixes with fluid to make semen. Erections and wet dreams can happen and are common. They are not something to joke about in the hallway.
  4. 4We name that pregnancy can become possible later when sperm and egg meet. How that happens, implantation, and birth are Grade 6. Grade 5 stops at capacity and the two signs.
  5. 5Everyone in the room learns both systems. This is science, not a boys’ lesson and a girls’ lesson.

Example story (read aloud)

The class used calm science voices. Menstruation is a cycle. A period is the lining leaving the body. It is not dirty and it is not a punishment. Sophie wrote: if I am unprepared at school I go to the office or a trusted adult. Pads are in more than one place so nobody has to announce it.

Ravi wrote: sperm is made in the testicles and semen is sperm plus fluid. Wet dreams can happen. You wash and you do not perform them as a joke.

Why does everyone learn both? Because these are human body systems, classmates deserve dignity, and you may need to be a respectful friend or a future trusted adult. Capacity is not a green light. It is biology.

12 min

Activity / worksheet

Two-column process notes + hygiene + “everyone learns both.” Private questions go in the box.
