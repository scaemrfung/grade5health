Alberta PEW Grade 5

Healthy Relationships

Organizing idea
Personal well-being is supported through positive relationships built on communication, collaboration, empathy, and respect.
Guiding question
How can communication and listening skills support healthy relationships?
Learning outcome
Students acknowledge and connect perspectives of self and others through communication and listening skills.
  • Describe how experiences affect perspectives
  • Use listening and communication
  • Reach shared understanding
  • Work respectfully on a task
