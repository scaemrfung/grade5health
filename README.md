# Grade 5 Health — weekly 30-minute PEW lessons

Patrick Fung · Elk Island Public Schools · 2026–2027

Static HTML. Nothing to install. Do not need a server.

## Open locally

- **Mac:** double-click `index.html`, or in Terminal: `open index.html`
- **Any computer:** drag `index.html` into Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox
- Keep the folder together (`css/`, `js/`, and the unit pages next to `index.html`)

## Teach tomorrow

1. Open `index.html` for the year map.
2. Open this week’s unit page.
3. Expand the lesson. Read the example story in the 8-minute teach. Photocopy or use **Print this worksheet**. Do not photocopy the teacher sample answers.
4. Timebox: 5 warm-up · 8 teach · 12 worksheet · 5 close.

## Files

- `index.html` — hub
- `character.html` — Weeks 1–6
- `relationships.html` — Weeks 7–12
- `safety.html` — Weeks 13–18
- `nutrition.html` — Weeks 19–24
- `growth.html` — Weeks 25–30
- `letter.html` — family letter (send before Week 25)
- `financial.html` — Weeks 31–36
- `css/styles.css`, `js/site.js`
- `pdfs/` — printable student worksheets (letter size), one PDF per unit

Open a unit page and use **Download student worksheets (PDF)**, or grab all six from the home page. Packets are student worksheets only (no teacher answers, stories, or lesson plans).

36 lessons. One 30-minute block per week. Aligned to Alberta Grade 5 PEW wellness outcomes. Not an official Alberta Education document.
