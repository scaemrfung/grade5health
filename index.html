The 30-minute block

  1. 5 min

    Warm-up

    One question or short scenario. Two answers collected.

  2. 8 min

    Teach

    Numbered steps. Board notes stay simple. One example story, plus an optional faith note.

  3. 12 min

    Worksheet

    Independent or pairs. Short enough to finish. Collect or stamp.

  4. 5 min

    Close

    Check for understanding. One sentence out the door. Preview next week.
